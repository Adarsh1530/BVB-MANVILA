Steering Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir Manvila with distinguished public service and educational expertise.

CHAIRMAN

T. Balakrishnan IAS (R)

Chairman's Message

Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan has consistently fostered the intellectual and cultural renaissance of India. Our institutions are not merely centers for imparting factual syllabi, but sacred crucibles where character, patriotism, and reverence for Dharma are molded into students.

At Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir, Manvila, our faculty and management committee tirelessly uphold these ideals, ensuring that modern CBSE pedagogy is enriched by traditional moral wisdom.

PRINCIPAL

Smt. Deepa V

Principal's Message

“Intelligence plus character, that is the goal of true education” – Martin Luther King Jr.

This quote becomes the definition of true education and it points out to the need of imparting values to build strong character. Values add quality to life. Any thought, idea or feeling which promotes self-development of an individual constitutes a value. Academic excellence and moral values together will help the child to excel in his life.

We Bhavanites impart value based education and also equip our children with social and relationship skills that help them throughout their lives. It is also important to create a positive learning environment. The environment is always a decisive factor, the one that will be limiting or nurturing a child’s potential. We are keen to provide our children an atmosphere where creativity is recognized and respected.

Education today is not just confined to textbooks and classrooms. Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan encourages our students to participate in extracurricular activities and also, we ensure the well-being and happiness of each student. We provide the right atmosphere for learners to develop their soft skills and empowers them to be critical thinkers and life long learners. Our aim is to make the child a better human being. Let’s move together with confidence, pride and enthusiasm.
