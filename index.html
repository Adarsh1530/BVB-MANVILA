OUR JOURNEY & CAMPUS ENVIRONMENT

OUR JOURNEY & CAMPUS FOR DISCOVERY

Trace the verified journey of Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir from its 1938 founding vision and 1995 inception into a serene 2.89-acre green academic haven in Manvila.

📜 Historical Milestones

1938

Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan Founded

Founded on November 7, 1938, by Kulapati Dr. K.M. Munshi with the blessings of Mahatma Gandhi as an intellectual, cultural, and educational movement.

1986

Thiruvananthapuram Kendra Established

Established under the illustrious helmsmanship of late Sri Sooranadu Kunjan Pillai and Secretary S.M. Radhakrishnan Nair.

1995

Humble Beginnings at Kumarapuram

Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir began in April 1995 in a rented building at Kumarapuram with classes I to V, fewer than 100 students, and 9 staff members.

2003

Permanent Campus at Manvila

Shifted on June 16, 2003, with 247 students and 24 staff members to the serene newly constructed 2.89-acre campus in Manvila.

2007–08

First AISSE (Class X) Board Batch

The first Class X batch of 29 students appeared for the March 2008 CBSE Board Examinations, securing 100% pass with 21 first-class distinctions.

2011

First Senior Secondary Commerce Batch

Commenced the Senior Secondary Commerce stream; the inaugural batch secured 100% pass in the 2012–13 AISSCE examinations.

2012–13

First Science Stream Batch

Launched Senior Secondary Science streams (Computer Science & Biology/Maths) with dedicated modern laboratory facilities.

Present

Sustained Academic Excellence

Celebrating over 30 years of holistic value-based education with consistent 100% CBSE board results, smart classrooms, and vibrant student life.

Principal's Message

Smt. Deepa V — Principal, BVB Manvila

“Intelligence plus character, that is the goal of true education” – Martin Luther King Jr.

This quote becomes the definition of true education and it points out to the need of imparting values to build strong character. Values add quality to life. Any thought, idea or feeling which promotes self-development of an individual constitutes a value. Academic excellence and moral values together will help the child to excel in his life.

We Bhavanites impart value based education and also equip our children with social and relationship skills that help them throughout their lives. It is also important to create a positive learning environment. The environment is always a decisive factor, the one that will be limiting or nurturing a child’s potential. We are keen to provide our children an atmosphere where creativity is recognized and respected.

Education today is not just confined to textbooks and classrooms. Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan encourages our students to participate in extracurricular activities and also, we ensure the well-being and happiness of each student. We provide the right atmosphere for learners to develop their soft skills and empowers them to be critical thinkers and life long learners.

Our aim is to make the child a better human being. Let’s move together with confidence, pride and enthusiasm.
