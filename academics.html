SENIOR SECONDARY

SCIENCE & COMMERCE STREAMS

In-depth curricula supported by fully equipped labs and experienced postgraduate educators.

CBSE SENIOR SECONDARY

SCIENCE STREAM

Designed for students aspiring towards careers in Medicine, Engineering, Biotechnology, Computer Science, and Pure Research.

  • Physics: Mechanics, Electrodynamics, Optics & Modern Physics with full practicals.
  • Chemistry: Organic, Inorganic & Physical Chemistry with titration and salt analysis.
  • Mathematics / Biology: Calculus, Algebra, Probability or Genetics, Human Physiology & Botany.
  • Computer Science: Python Programming, SQL Database Management, Data Structures & Networking.
  • English Core: Advanced comprehension, analytical writing, and literature.
CBSE SENIOR SECONDARY

COMMERCE STREAM

Cultivating financial literacy, corporate leadership, and managerial acumen with a 100% board examination record.

  • Accountancy: Partnership Accounting, Company Accounts & Financial Analysis.
  • Business Studies: Principles of Management, Financial Markets, Marketing & Consumer Protection.
  • Economics: Introductory Microeconomics, Macroeconomics & Indian Economic Development.
  • Informatics Practices / Applied Math: Data Handling with Python/Pandas, SQL Queries or Commercial Mathematics.
  • English Core: Formal business communication, debate, and literary studies.
Important Admission Note: Current senior secondary batches operate in Science and Commerce streams. Prospective candidates inquiring about Humanities combinations are requested to confirm ongoing batch schedules directly with the school principal's office.
