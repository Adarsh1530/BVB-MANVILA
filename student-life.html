Democratic Student Parliament & Cabinet Leadership

DEMOCRATIC GOVERNANCE

SCHOOL PARLIAMENT & LEADERSHIP

At Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir, student leadership is practiced through the School Parliament. Modeled after the Westminster parliamentary democracy, student elections are held through secret ballot voting.

The elected cabinet includes the Head Boy, Head Girl, Sports Captain, Cultural Secretary, and House Prefects who formally take oath during the annual Investiture Ceremony. They collaborate closely with the faculty to supervise morning assemblies, campus discipline, cleanliness drives, and inter-house events.
