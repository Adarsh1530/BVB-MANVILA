About Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir

Home / About Us

1995

Founded at Kumarapuram, now thriving on 2.89 green acres in Manvila

INSTITUTIONAL HERITAGE

AN ADVENTURE IN FAITH

Founded on November 7, 1938, by Kulapati Dr. K.M. Munshi with the blessings of Mahatma Gandhi, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan stands as an apolitical, cultural, and educational movement rooted in India's timeless heritage.

"Development of intellectual life of the students is not enough. It must be harnessed to moral and spiritual development. Information should blossom into knowledge, and knowledge should mature into wisdom."

The Thiruvananthapuram Kendra was established in 1986 under the leadership of late Sri Sooranadu Kunjan Pillai and Secretary S.M. Radhakrishnan Nair. In April 1995, Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir began in a rented premise at Kumarapuram with classes I to V, under 100 students, and 9 dedicated teachers.

On June 16, 2003, the school relocated to its permanent 2.89-acre campus at Manvila with 247 students and 24 staff members. Today, the campus features three modern blocks (Bhishma, Vyasa, and Mahatma Gandhi) nurturing students from LKG to XII under CBSE.

OUR EVOLUTION & HERITAGE

OUR JOURNEY & CAMPUS FOR DISCOVERY

Tracing our growth from a modest beginning to a premier 3.5-acre green academic haven in Manvila.

1995 – 2003: FOUNDATIONAL YEARS

The Genesis at Kumarapuram

Bhavan's Vivekananda Vidya Mandir commenced its noble journey in April 1995 at Kumarapuram, Thiruvananthapuram, with Classes I to V under 100 students and 9 dedicated teachers. Built on Kulapati K.M. Munshi's ideals of value-rooted holistic education, the school rapidly gained trust for academic discipline and cultural values.

2003 – PRESENT: MANVILA CAMPUS

Expansion to Manvila Campus

On June 16, 2003, the school relocated to its permanent, serene campus at Manvila with 247 students and 24 staff members. Today, the campus encompasses three state-of-the-art academic blocks (Bhishma, Vyasa, and Mahatma Gandhi) nurturing students from LKG through Senior Secondary Level (Science & Commerce).

ENVIRONMENT & EXPLORATION

A Campus for Discovery

Set amid lush greenery in Manvila, our campus is designed as an inspiring natural environment for scientific inquiry, athletic vitality, and cultural blossom. Features include the Nakshathravanam (Astrological Botanical Garden), digital smart classrooms, modern physics/chemistry/biology/computer laboratories, and multi-sport playfields.

OUR PURPOSE

VISION & MISSION

Guiding principles that define everyday life and education at Bhavan's Manvila.

Our Vision

To be a premier institution of holistic learning that nurtures intellectually competent, morally upright, culturally rooted, and socially sensitive citizens committed to global harmony and human welfare under the ideal of Vasudhaiva Kudumbakam (The World is One Family).

Our Mission

  • Provide value-based CBSE education from foundational kindergarten through senior secondary levels.
  • Cultivate critical inquiry, scientific temper, and experiential mastery in Science and Commerce.
  • Promote holistic development through Karate, Yoga, Chess, Performing Arts, and active student clubs.
  • Instill democratic leadership, civic awareness, and environmental stewardship through Nakshathravanam.
