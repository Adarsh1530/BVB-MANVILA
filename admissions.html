BEGIN YOUR JOURNEY AT BHAVAN'S

STEP-BY-STEP

ADMISSION PROCEDURE

A straightforward four-step process for prospective parents and students.

1

Online Registration

Fill out the digital application on the AMS ERP admission portal with student and guardian details.

2

Document Submission

Submit verified copies of birth certificate, previous school progress report, and transfer certificate.

3

Interaction / Assessment

An informal interaction for kindergarten or an academic readiness evaluation for senior classes.

4

Enrollment & Onboarding

Confirmation of admission, fee remittance, uniform allocation, and transport route assignment.

Documents Checklist

  • Original & attested copy of Birth Certificate (for KG & Class I).
  • Original Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous recognized school.
  • Report card / Mark statement of the qualifying examination.
  • Recent passport-size photographs of the student and parents.
  • Aadhaar card copy of student and parents for CBSE registration.

Fee Structure Guidelines

School tuition fees and annual development fees are levied as prescribed by the School Management Committee and Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan Kendra norms, compliant with regulatory directives. Fees are paid in installments via the online ERP portal.

For the official certified fee schedule document for the 2027–28 academic session, parents can download the brochure from the official portal or contact the accounts desk.
